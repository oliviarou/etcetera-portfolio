THE 3-BREATH RESET
A neuroscience-backed technique for leaders who need to regulate in real time — not on a meditation cushion.
When To Use This
  • Before a high-stakes meeting or difficult conversation
  • After a stressful interaction — before you send that email
  • When your chest tightens, jaw clenches, or thoughts race
  • In the car before walking into your house after work
  • Anytime you're reacting instead of responding
The 3-Breath Reset
01

NOTICE

Regulate. Inhale 4 counts, exhale 6. The longer exhale calms the system. Notice where the tension lives.

02

NAME

Reflect. Same breath. Ask: "What just activated me?" Move from autopilot to awareness.

03

CHOOSE

Reconnect. Same breath. Ask: "What do I actually want here?" Reactive becomes intentional.

PDF · Back Page
The Neuroscience
  • 0.07s — the amygdala triggers the stress response faster than conscious thought.
  • Extended exhale activates the vagus nerve, downshifting the system.
  • Conscious attention re-engages the prefrontal cortex for clear thinking.

This isn't relaxation — it's neurological regulation. It works in a boardroom, not just a yoga studio.
